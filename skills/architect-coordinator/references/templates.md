# Coordination Templates

Use only the fields needed for the task. Keep one shared contract rather than
copying divergent requirements into every assignment.

## Scope contract

```text
Outcome:
Non-goals:
Authoritative sources and settled decisions:
Compatibility and failure behavior:
Proposed boundaries / mechanisms to reuse:
Environment and repository instructions:
Acceptance evidence:
Reviewability expectation:
Authorization / next stop:
Unresolved material decisions:
```

## Agent assignment

```text
Role and bounded objective:
Shared contract location:
Owned paths/behavior; excluded areas:
Base/head and dependencies:
Required investigation or brief design before edits:
Validation and report location:
Stop/escalate conditions:

Handoff: changed paths and head, behavior/design summary, checks and limitations,
production/test/doc footprint, remaining findings or dependencies.
```

## Evidence ledger

| Workstream | Owner | Base → head | Dependency | Status / finding | Evidence / next gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Record integration against the final assembled head. Include independent-review
closeout, actual cross-boundary validation, and publication verification when
applicable. A passing component selection does not imply full-system acceptance.
